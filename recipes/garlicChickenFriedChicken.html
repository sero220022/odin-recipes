Garlic Chicken Fried Chicken

Home

Description

crispy, deep-fried chicken where a heavy, punchy garlic flavor is the star.

features a heavily-seasoned flour or starch crust and is often either marinated in or drizzled with a savory garlic butter or infused garlic oil.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ cup seasoned bread crumbs
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves - pounded thin
  • 1 cup oil for frying, or as needed

Steps:

  1. In a shallow dish, mix together the garlic powder, pepper, salt, paprika, bread crumbs and flour. In a separate dish, whisk together the milk and egg.
  2. Heat the oil in an electric skillet set to 350°F (175°C) . Dip the chicken into the egg and milk, then dredge in the dry ingredients until evenly coated.
  3. Fry chicken in the hot oil for about 5 minutes per side, or until the chicken is cooked through and juices run clear. Remove from the oil with a slotted spatula, and serve.
